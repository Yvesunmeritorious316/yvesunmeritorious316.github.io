## 🎨 How to Use the Tool

This tool simplifies the process of removing backgrounds. Follow this guide to edit your first image.

### Loading Files
Open the program and click the Add Files button. You can choose a single photo or select an entire folder. The program displays your files in a list on the main window. You can drag and drop images directly into this area to add them to your workspace.

### Removing Backgrounds
The program automatically detects the main subject in every image. Click the Process button to let the AI handle the segmentation. The software applies an alpha matte to ensure the edges around hair or complex objects look smooth. You can see the result in the preview window before you save your work.

### Adjusting Settings
If the program misses a part of the image, use the manual correction brush. Increase the brush size to cover large areas or decrease it for delicate edges. You can toggle the view between the original image and the edited version to compare the results.

### Saving Your Work
Choose the target folder for your images. You can save your files in different formats, such as PNG or JPG. The PNG format preserves the transparency, which is helpful if you plan to place your subject on a new background later. Click the Export button to save all processed images to your computer.

## 📁 Working with Multiple Files
